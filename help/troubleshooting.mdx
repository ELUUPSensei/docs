If something is not working as expected in ELUUP AI, check the symptoms below for the most likely cause and steps to resolve it.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot log in">
    **Likely cause**: incorrect password, unverified email, or browser issue.

    **Resolution**:

    1. Make sure Caps Lock is off and you are typing the correct password.
    2. If you forgot your password, click **Forgot password** on the sign-in page and follow the reset email.
    3. Check that you verified your email address after signing up.
    4. Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try again in an incognito window.
    5. If you use a password manager, confirm it filled the correct credentials.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="AI task results are slow">
    **Likely cause**: large input size, high platform load, or a complex model.

    **Resolution**:

    1. Reduce the size of the input file or split it into smaller batches.
    2. Check the [status page](https://status.eluup.ai) for any reported slowdowns.
    3. Switch to a lighter AI model if your task does not require the most advanced option.
    4. If you are on the Free plan, consider upgrading to Pro or Business for higher priority processing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Integration shows a connection error">
    **Likely cause**: expired credentials, changed permissions, or a network restriction.

    **Resolution**:

    1. Open the integration in ELUUP AI and click **Reconnect**.
    2. Re-authenticate with the third-party service and grant all requested permissions.
    3. Verify that your account on the third-party service is active and has not been restricted.
    4. Check whether your organization uses IP allowlists and add ELUUP AI if needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Automation is not triggering">
    **Likely cause**: automation is disabled, trigger conditions are not met, or a task limit was reached.

    **Resolution**:

    1. Open the automation and confirm it is **enabled**.
    2. Review the trigger settings (schedule, event, or webhook) and test the condition manually if possible.
    3. Check **Settings** \> **Billing** to see if you have reached your monthly AI task or active automation limit.
    4. Review recent automation run logs for error messages and fix the reported issue.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Model output is unexpected or incorrect">
    **Likely cause**: unclear instructions, low-quality input data, or a model mismatch.

    **Resolution**:

    1. Rewrite your prompt to be more specific. Include examples of the desired output format.
    2. Clean the input data: remove irrelevant rows, fix formatting issues, and ensure consistent structure.
    3. Try a different AI model that is better suited to your task type.
    4. Run a small test sample first to tune the prompt before processing the full dataset.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
