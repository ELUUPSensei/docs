Get quick answers to the most common questions about using ELUUP AI. If you do not find what you need, see the related articles below or contact support.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is ELUUP AI and what does it do?">
    ELUUP AI is an AI-powered SaaS platform that helps individuals and teams automate workflows, manage projects, and generate intelligent insights from their data. You connect your data sources, set up AI models, and let the platform handle repetitive tasks and analysis.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does ELUUP AI handle data privacy?">
    Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. ELUUP AI does not use your workspace data to train third-party AI models, and you can request a full export or deletion of your data at any time from Account settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What file types are supported?">
    ELUUP AI supports CSV, JSON, JSONL, PDF, TXT, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, and common image formats (PNG, JPG, WEBP). Individual files can be up to 100 MB on Pro and Business plans, and 10 MB on the Free plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How accurate are the AI model outputs?">
    Accuracy depends on the model, the quality of your input data, and how well instructions are written. Results are meant to assist your judgment, not replace it. Always review outputs before using them in production decisions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are automations billed?">
    Each time an automation runs and uses an AI task, it counts against your monthly task limit. Triggers themselves (such as a schedule or webhook) are free. Only the AI processing step consumes tasks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export my results?">
    Yes. You can download project outputs and automation results as CSV, JSON, or PDF from each project page. Business plans also support scheduled exports to cloud storage integrations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between a team workspace and a personal workspace?">
    A personal workspace belongs to you and is private by default. A team workspace is shared and includes role-based permissions so teammates can collaborate on projects and automations under a single billing account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Pro users can email [support@eluup.ai](mailto:support@eluup.ai). Business plans also have access to in-app chat. Before reaching out, check [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) for common fixes and include your workspace name and any error text.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel my subscription at any time?">
    Yes. You can downgrade to the Free plan or cancel entirely from **Settings** \> **Billing**. Your workspace remains active until the end of the current billing period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does ELUUP AI offer a free trial?">
    The Free plan includes core features with limited usage so you can evaluate the platform. If you need a higher tier for evaluation, contact sales for a Business trial.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
