ELUUP AI is an autonomous AI trading agent platform for DEX altcoins. Our agents trade directly on decentralized exchanges using proprietary buy and sell signals, with built-in risk management that protects your capital on every trade. The platform currently supports Base chain and is expanding to BNB Chain and Solana.

Whether you are a trader looking to automate your strategy, a DeFi developer building on-chain tools, or an AI agent builder integrating trading capabilities, ELUUP AI provides the APIs, SDKs, and infrastructure you need to deploy intelligent trading agents.

## How it works

Deploying a profitable trading agent takes three steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your API key">
    Sign up at [app.eluup.ai](https://app.eluup.ai) and generate an API key from your account settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy an agent">
    Use the API or dashboard to create an agent with your preferred risk settings and trading strategy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect profits">
    Your agent monitors proprietary signals, enters high-probability trades, and manages exits automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Core capabilities

- **Autonomous trading agents** that execute buy and sell orders on DEXs without manual intervention
- **Proprietary signal data** generated by AI models trained on on-chain and market data
- **Built-in risk management** including position sizing, stop loss, trailing stops, and take profit on every trade
- **Multi-chain support** starting with Base (chain ID 8453) and expanding to BNB Chain (56) and Solana
- **MCP server** for integrating trading capabilities into AI agent frameworks and toolchains
