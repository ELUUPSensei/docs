ELUUP AI offers three plan tiers to match how you and your team work. Review the feature comparisons below, then choose or change your plan directly from Billing settings.

## Plan comparison

| Feature | Free | Pro | Business |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workspaces | 1 | 5 | Unlimited |
| Team members | 2 | 10 | Unlimited |
| AI tasks per month | 100 | 5,000 | 50,000 |
| Automations | 3 active | 20 active | Unlimited |
| Integrations | Basic | Basic \+ advanced | All integrations |
| Priority support | No | Email | Email \+ chat |
| Custom AI model tuning | No | No | Yes |
| SSO | No | No | Yes |

<Note>
  Annual discount: save up to 20% by switching to yearly billing on Pro or Business plans.
</Note>

## Change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Click your avatar in the top-right corner, select **Settings**, then go to the **Billing** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a plan">
    Choose **Free**, **Pro**, or **Business** and select monthly or annual billing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm payment">
    Enter or confirm your payment method and click **Subscribe**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you upgrade mid-cycle, your new plan starts immediately and you are charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the billing period. If you downgrade, the change takes effect at the start of the next cycle.
</Warning>

## Manage your payment method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open payment methods">
    In **Billing**, select the **Payment methods** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or update a card">
    Click **Add card** or select an existing card and click **Update**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a default">
    Mark your preferred card as the default for future charges.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A failed payment may suspend automations or pause workspace creation. Update your payment method promptly if you receive a billing alert.
</Warning>

## Understand usage limits

Your plan controls how many AI tasks and active automations you can run each month.

- **AI tasks**: counted each time an AI model processes a request in a project or automation.
- **Active automations**: the number of automations set to run automatically. Disabled automations do not count.
- **Team members**: the total number of invited users with accepted invitations.

When you approach your limit, ELUUP AI sends an email alert. You can monitor current usage in **Settings** \> **Billing**.

<Tip>
  If you need more tasks temporarily, upgrade to Pro or Business and downgrade later. The change takes effect at your next billing cycle.
</Tip>
