Teams in ELUUP AI let you share workspaces, collaborate on projects, and control who can configure automations or manage billing. The workspace Owner or an Admin invites people and assigns roles.

## Invite a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team settings">
    Click your avatar in the top-right corner, select **Settings**, then go to the **Team** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send an invitation">
    Click **Invite member**, enter the person's email address, and choose a role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Send invitation**. The teammate receives an email with a link to join.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Pending invitations appear in the member list until the person accepts. You can resend or cancel an invitation at any time.
</Note>

## Role permissions

Each role controls what a teammate can see and change.

| Permission | Owner | Admin | Member | Viewer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| View projects | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Create and edit projects | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Run automations | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Manage integrations | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Invite and remove members | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Manage billing and plans | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Delete workspace | Yes | No | No | No |
| Transfer ownership | Yes | No | No | No |

<Warning>
  Only Owners can transfer ownership or delete the workspace. Assign Admin roles to teammates who need to help manage members or billing without full ownership rights.
</Warning>

## Remove a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the member list">
    Go to **Settings** \> **Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the member">
    Click the **...** menu next to the member's name and select **Remove from team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Remove** to finalize. The person loses access immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Removing a member does not delete their personal account or any of their own workspaces. It only revokes access to your workspace.
</Tip>

## Transfer workspace ownership

When you transfer ownership, the new Owner gains full control and your role changes to Admin.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the new Owner">
    In **Settings** \> **Team**, find the member you want to promote and open their **...** menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer ownership">
    Select **Transfer ownership** and enter your password to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your new role">
    After the transfer, your role becomes Admin. You can still manage the workspace, but you can no longer delete it or transfer it again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Ownership transfer is immediate and cannot be undone by support. Make sure the new Owner is a trusted teammate.
</Warning>
