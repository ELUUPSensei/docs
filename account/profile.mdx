Your ELUUP AI profile controls how you appear to teammates and how the platform communicates with you. Keep your contact details current, choose which alerts you receive, and manage API keys for programmatic access.

## Update your profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your avatar in the top-right corner and select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your display name">
    Under **Profile**, update your **Display name** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload an avatar">
    Click your avatar image, choose a new photo, and crop if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click **Save** to apply your updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change your email address

Your email is used for sign-in and all platform notifications.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open account settings">
    In **Settings**, go to the **Account** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a new email">
    Type your new email address into the **Email** field and click **Update email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the change">
    Check your inbox for a confirmation message and click the verification link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  You must verify the new email before it takes effect. Until then, sign in with your current address.
</Warning>

## Manage notification preferences

Control how and when ELUUP AI reaches out to you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open notifications">
    In **Settings**, select the **Notifications** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose channels">
    Toggle **Email** and **In-app** alerts for each category: project updates, automation runs, team invitations, and billing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save preferences">
    Click **Save preferences** to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Enable email alerts for automations so you are notified if a run fails.
</Tip>

## Manage API keys

API keys let external tools and scripts access your workspaces and projects on your behalf.

### Generate a key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open API keys">
    In **Settings**, go to the **API keys** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a key">
    Click **Generate new key**, enter a descriptive label, and select the permissions you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy and store">
    Copy the key immediately. ELUUP AI displays it only once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Revoke a key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the key">
    In **API keys**, locate the key you want to remove.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revoke">
    Click the **Revoke** button and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Revoking a key breaks any integration or script using it. Update those tools with a new key before revoking.
</Warning>
