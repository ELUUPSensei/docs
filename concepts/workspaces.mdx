A workspace in ELUUP AI is a shared environment where you organize projects, connect data sources, and run AI tasks. Everything inside a workspace, documents, integrations, automation history, and model outputs, stays scoped to that workspace so your teams and projects stay cleanly separated.

Think of a workspace as a digital office for a team or initiative. When you invite collaborators, they get access to everything inside that workspace based on their role, but they cannot see into other workspaces unless you explicitly add them there. This makes it easy to run a marketing workspace and a product workspace side by side without mixing data or permissions.

## How data and AI tasks are scoped

Every data source you connect, file you upload, and automation you build lives inside a single workspace. AI tasks use only the data sources and configurations from that workspace, which keeps outputs relevant and secure. If you need the same data in two places, you can connect the source again inside another workspace, or you can invite collaborators into one shared workspace instead.

## Personal workspaces vs team workspaces

- **Personal workspace**: Created automatically when you sign up. Only you have access. It is ideal for private experiments, drafts, and testing new automations before sharing them with your team.
- **Team workspace**: Created by you or an admin and shared with invited members. Team workspaces support role-based access, shared project views, and collaborative automation building.

<Note>
  Personal workspaces are available on all plans. Additional team workspaces may be limited by your current plan. Check your billing settings to see how many team workspaces you can create.
</Note>

When you are ready to move from solo experimentation to team collaboration, you can invite members into your personal workspace or create a dedicated team workspace to keep permissions and data cleanly separated.
