ELUUP AI is built around three core ideas that work together to turn your data into action. Understanding how Workspaces, AI Models, and Automations connect will help you get the most out of the platform from your very first project.

A Workspace is the container where your team organizes projects and data sources. Inside that container, you choose AI Models to analyze, generate, or transform content. Then you wire everything together with Automations that run on a schedule or react to events, so the work keeps happening even when you are away.

When you start a new initiative, you begin by creating a Workspace. You then connect data sources and pick the AI Models best suited for your goals. Finally, you build Automations so the platform processes new data, sends alerts, or generates reports without manual prompting. This loop is the heart of the ELUUP AI experience.
