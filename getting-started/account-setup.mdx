sidebarTitle: "Account Setup"
description: "Get your ELUUP AI account ready with email verification, profile completion, two-factor authentication, and your first team invitation in a few simple steps."
---

Your ELUUP AI account is the gateway to building AI-powered workflows. Follow the steps below to finish setup and secure your workspace so you can start collaborating with your team right away.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    After signing up, check your inbox for a verification email from ELUUP AI. Click the **Verify Email** button inside the message. If you do not see it within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.

    Once verified, you will be redirected to the ELUUP AI dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Open **Account Settings** from the top-right menu. Add your full name, job title, and a profile photo. Keeping your profile up to date helps teammates identify you in shared workspaces and on activity timelines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable two-factor authentication">
    Go to **Security** in Account Settings and toggle on **Two-Factor Authentication**. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, enter the generated code, and save your recovery codes in a secure location.

    <Warning>
      Store your recovery codes somewhere safe. If you lose access to your authenticator app, these codes are the only way back into your account.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your first team member">
    From the dashboard, click **Invite Team Member**. Enter your colleague's email address and assign a role (Admin, Editor, or Viewer). They will receive an invitation email to join your workspace.

    <Tip>
      You can always manage or revoke invitations later from the Team Management page.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

That is all. Your account is secure, your profile is complete, and your first teammate has been invited. You are now ready to create your first project in ELUUP AI.
